SDSU Climate Dynamics Animation Library

Our library is a reproduction of the University of Washington General Circulation Animations Library, by Professor John Michael Wallace.

Data Source

All animations are based on ERA5 Reanalysis Data from the Copernicus Climate Data Store (CDS), covering the period 1979–2000. Each frame represents a weekly mean (weeks starting on the 1st, 8th, 15th, and 22nd of each month).

Lightning

Annual lightning stroke density.

Data Source

This animation is based on the WWLLN Global Lightning Climatology (WGLC), derived from the World Wide Lightning Location Network (WWLLN), covering 2010–2025. Each frame represents a calendar-day climatological mean (Feb 29 excluded), annualized to strokes km-2 yr-1 to match the reference product's units.

About: WWLLN/WGLC Annual Lightning Stroke Density 2010–2025. Shows the climatology of lightning stroke density, annualized to strokes per km² per year. Each date marks a calendar-day climatological mean (Feb 29 excluded).
